PROPOSAL HD—002 · REV C

This one actually is built on the v0 foundation

  • shadcn "stone" tokens, verbatim. Background, card, border, muted-foreground — the exact neutral scale v0 generates with (stone-50 ground, stone-200 hairlines, stone-500 secondary text). The warm stone scale was chosen over cold zinc because it quietly keeps the old-money warmth.
  • v0 component recipes: the badge pill with status dot, primary/outline button pair with shadow-sm, bento grid with icon tiles and metadata chips, sticky blurred navbar with a CTA button in it, dot-grid hero background (which here doubles as graph paper — the one place engineering and SaaS aesthetics are the same thing).
  • Sans-first typography. Headline is a tight-tracked modern sans, not a serif. Exactly one serif italic phrase survives, in racing green — that flourish plus the gilt logo mark is the entire "old money" budget on this page. In the real build: Geist or Inter, self-hosted.
  • Racing green is the primary button colour — where v0 would put black. After dark it hands over to gilt. That single substitution is what makes it yours rather than a template.
  • The lamp stays — now a proper shadcn icon-button in the navbar. Try it: the whole page moves through stone-950, and green passes the torch to gold.
  • The gear train is back in the hero — the exact mechanism from the current site (same tooth geometry, mesh phasing and drag physics), recoloured from engraved gilt to the token palette: stone wireframe with racing-green depth in daylight, gold after dark. Sitting on the dot grid it reads as a technical figure, captioned FIG. 01 in mono. Drag it; then flick the lamp while it spins.
  • Kept from the heritage site: instrument numbering in mono, the diamond mark, the colophon line, tabular figures. Retired: frames, ruled paper, small-caps letterspacing, italic headings, "Est. MMXXVI".
