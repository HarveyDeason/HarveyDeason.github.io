# Third-party assets

## bookend-thinker.webp

Rendered from a 3D mesh scan of Auguste Rodin's *The Thinker* (Musée Rodin,
Paris), scanned and published by **Scan The World**, via **Sketchfab**.

Rodin died in 1917, so the sculpture itself is in the public domain. The *scan*
is a separate work with its own terms — Scan The World publish under Creative
Commons, which requires attribution. Credit is shown to readers on
`/writing/` beneath the bookcase, not only here.

The image in this repo is a derivative: an orthographic render of the mesh at a
270° yaw with a z-buffer and Lambert shading, recoloured to a dark cast finish
and exported as WebP. The source STL is not committed.

If the exact Sketchfab URL or licence variant is confirmed later, add it here
and to the on-page credit.
