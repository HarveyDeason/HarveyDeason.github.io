About

Harvey Deason

Harvey Deason is a digital delivery engineer in the UK water industry, based near Bath. He came up through an engineering team — a mechanical design apprenticeship, then six years producing models, general arrangements and fabrication drawings — before moving into digital delivery, first as a BIM engineer at Wessex Water and now at YTL Construction in Bath.

His work sits where the drawing office meets the site: 3D design and process drawings for the water utilities, utilizing point-cloud surveys,drawing P&IDs, as-builts, and design packs held to WIMES and British Standards. He is currently completing a top-up Bachelor of Engineering with the Open University.

This site is his workshop and his journal in one place: a cabinet of engineering instruments he builds for the trade, alongside a journal of essays on work, output, and the well-considered life.

Away from the desk he builds for the pleasure of it — 3D printing and engineering personal projects that bring mechatronic, electrical and mechanical considerations together in one design. The instruments are practical and browser-based. The writing is thinking-in-progress.
